<p>I am a first-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at the <strong>HKUST NLP Group</strong>, advised by <strong>Professor Junxian He</strong>. My research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with a particular emphasis on understanding and improving large language models.</p>

  <li>LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability</li>
</ul>
